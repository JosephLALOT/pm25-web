What is PM2.5

PM2.5 are fine particles smaller than 2.5 micrometres, about 30 times thinner than a hair. They come mainly from traffic, industry and burning. They go deep into the lungs and the blood, and long exposure raises the risk of heart and lung disease.

Where the data comes from

Current values, history and the CAMS forecast come from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API. Our server fetches them every hour and keeps them in memory.

How we forecast

Our model was trained by the data science part of the team on past PM2.5 values. Every hour, it predicts the next hours and days for each city. We show it next to the CAMS forecast so you can compare both.

Limits

Modelled, not measured. Outside Europe, values come from the CAMS global model at about 45 km resolution. They are estimates for the area, not readings from a ground station.

If our model is unavailable, the site automatically shows the CAMS forecast instead.
